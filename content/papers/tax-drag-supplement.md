The 20.89% scalar rate is flat by construction. The actual rises from 12%, crosses in 2045, and pins at 22%. Deposits cross in the same year. Six larger actual deposits come first, followed by fourteen smaller ones. Early larger deposits receive more shelter, so their greater weight more than offsets later shortfalls. That asymmetry difference is \$63.

The exact rate column averages 19.88% in present value over 2039–2058. The scalar rule applies $t_D$ = 20.89%, which is the PV-weighted rate over the full 2039–2068 schedule — a rate drawn from a wider window than the deposits occupy.
